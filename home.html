CUSTOMER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Intelligent Home predict power cuts?

Our IoT edge controller connects to the internet to ingest real-time Doppler meteorological radar feeds, storm tracks, and municipal load-shedding schedules. When severe weather or a grid cut is imminent, it engages Storage Defense Mode to pre-charge batteries to 100%.

Can SiddhiXTech build both hardware and software?

Yes. We have deep full-stack capabilities ranging from IoT firmware, inverter communication protocols, and energy sensors, up to mobile apps (Kotlin/Android), cloud APIs, and web dashboards.

Can existing solar or inverters be upgraded?

In many cases, yes. Our smart controller can interface with existing hybrid inverters and battery banks via RS485/Modbus or auxiliary relays to add predictive AI automation without replacing your existing panels.

How do I start a project with SiddhiXTech?

Simply reach out via our contact page or email us directly at support@siddhixtech.com. We schedule an engineering consultation to understand your requirements, evaluate site or software specs, and provide a clear timeline.
