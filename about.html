ABOUT SIDDHIXTECH

A technology ecosystem built around resilient living.

SiddhiXTech is focused on solving foundational human and business needs through technology — from autonomous, blackout-proof intelligent homes powered by hybrid energy and AI, to mission-critical software solutions.

Our Primary Focus

We believe the home of the future must be self-sufficient and resilient. By orchestrating Solar, Wind, Grid and Generator power through predictive weather and outage AI, we eliminate blackouts and empower households with total energy autonomy.

Our Core Principles

  • Real-world resilience: Practical energy independence over fragile systems
  • Predictive intelligence: Software that anticipates problems before they occur
  • Engineering depth: From IoT firmware and hardware integration to cloud platforms
  • Sustainable economy: Slashing energy bills and fossil-fuel consumption
“Siddhi” represents achievement and realization. Our mission is to turn ambitious engineering into everyday resilience.
