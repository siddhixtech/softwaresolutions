HOW THE SYSTEM THINKS

An autonomous 24/7 energy management cycle.

PHASE 1

Constant Environmental & Grid Monitoring

The SiddhiXTech home IoT controller continuously checks utility grid voltage stability, weather radar feeds, and solar/wind generation rates.

PHASE 2

Predictive Outage & Weather Alert

If approaching bad weather (high winds, heavy rain, lightning) or a scheduled government grid cut is detected, the AI immediately switches to "Outage Preparation Mode".

PHASE 3

Pre-Charge & Battery Protection

The system prioritizes full battery charge immediately, turns off high-drain non-essential loads, and locks in energy reserves so the inverter is never caught empty.

PHASE 4

Intelligent Hybrid Power Delivery

During the power cut, energy is delivered from solar/wind first, then conserved battery power. If battery levels hit a critical threshold during prolonged bad weather, the generator is started automatically, run efficiently to replenish storage, and then cleanly shut down.
