# SiddhiXTech Website

Official website for SiddhiXTech — focused primarily on **Intelligent Home & Clean Energy AI**, alongside high-end software development and digital products.

## Primary Focus: Intelligent Home & Energy AI
- **Hybrid Energy Synergy**: Seamless orchestration of Solar PV, Micro Wind Turbines, Utility Grid, and automated Petrol/Diesel Generators.
- **Predictive Weather & Grid Cut AI**: Real-time integration with meteorological Doppler radar and government load-shedding schedules to prepare before power cuts occur.
- **Inverter Storage Defense Mode**: Pre-charges battery storage before storms and locks out high-drain loads so your inverter is never caught empty during a blackout.
- **Smart Appliance Orchestration**: Automatically shifts heavy electrical loads (EV charging, water pumps, HVAC) to peak solar hours.
